FAQ

Common questions

How is this different from hiring an SDR?

An SDR is a person. We're a system — strategy, data, tooling, execution, and optimisation all in one. We're operational in 30 days, not 90. And you're not betting $120K+ on a single hire who might not work out.

What channels do you run?

Cold email, LinkedIn outreach, and cold calling — across all three or just the ones that make sense for your market. We run the full tech stack: Instantly, HeyReach, Clay, Apollo, and more.

How long until we see results?

First meetings typically land within 30 days of going live. The first two weeks is setup and targeting. By week three, campaigns are running and replies are coming in.

What do we actually need to do?

We need your input on ICP and positioning in week one. After that, you handle the meetings once they're booked. Everything in between — prospecting, messaging, sending, optimising — is on us.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope — which channels, what volume, and how much of the system you want us to own. Best to book a call and we'll figure out what makes sense for your situation.

Do you work with any industry?

We focus on B2B — SaaS, professional services, and high-ticket agencies. If you're selling to businesses and need more pipeline, there's a good chance we can help.
